## Validation assessment: Share with caveats

- No affordability conclusion without income, expenses, debts, retirement constraints and mortgage quote.
- Cash unit check: [số dư cá nhân đã ẩn]. No assumption that cash must remain untouched or all be used as downpayment.
- Monthly housing ratio includes debt service, ownership costs and repair allowance; recurring debt outside housing is checked separately through residual cash flow. Stress ratio denominator uses reduced income.
- Interest stress is 2 percentage POINTS, not 2 percent; only variable/resettable exposure. Income -20% and non-loan housing costs +30% are scenarios, not forecasts. Long fixed loans are not subjected to an immediate imaginary repricing.
- Emergency reserve target: 12 months essential post-purchase outflows, with known large liabilities separate. Not a claim that every household needs exactly this amount or is protected against every event.
- No inferred all-in cost or mortgage amount based only on purchase price minus current cash.
- Education and commute are secondary preferences. Their market-demand role is distinguished from personal convenience; prevent double counting and critical-risk offsets.
- Detached road-width/frontage preference is expressly not a legal test; rebuildability still needs case-specific verification.
- Condo age preference is soft, not an earthquake compliance cutoff, lending limit or universal investment rule. No assumption of rebuilding proceeds or wooden-house zero value.
- Three-to-five/ten-year prices and future appreciation are not manufactured. Proposed comparable sample count is an evidence target, not sufficient statistical proof.
- Rental fallback requires lender/other permissions and financial checks; not assumed available under an owner-occupier loan.
- No source conflicts requiring a quantitative reconciliation. Unavailable property documents prevent approval of a property, not publication of this decision framework.
