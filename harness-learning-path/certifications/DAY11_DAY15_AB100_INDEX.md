## Official AB-100 weighting

- Plan AI-powered business solutions: 25–30%
- Design AI-powered business solutions: 25–30%
- Deploy AI-powered business solutions: 40–45%

The first five AB-100 days intentionally emphasize planning/design judgment first. The remaining days will move into knowledge architecture, security, governance, ALM, monitoring, testing, telemetry, and ROI because deployment/operations is the largest exam domain.

## Block exit criteria

By the end of Day 15, explain an enterprise scenario through this chain:

```text
Business Goal
  ↓
AI Opportunity
  ↓
Build / Buy / Extend
  ↓
Microsoft Platform Composition
  ↓
Single / Multi-Agent Decision
  ↓
Tools / MCP / A2A
  ↓
Security Boundary
```

PASS if the architecture rationale is based on business fit, data, risk, integration, governance, cost, and operational complexity rather than product preference.
